THE 7-STEP READ

Never guess the rhythm first.

  1. RateFast, normal or slow?
  2. RhythmRegular or irregular?
  3. P wavesPresent? One before every QRS?
  4. PR intervalNormal, prolonged or changing?
  5. QRSNarrow or wide? Morphology?
  6. AxisLead I and aVF.
  7. ST–TIschaemia, injury, electrolytes.
